CFexpress cards

These are NVMe drives, so they keep their own health log: data read and written, life used, power cycles. Your card reader has to pass that log through. You can check this once with smartctl.

SD cards

SD cards keep no wear log a reader can see. The app counts what it sees itself: imports, data imported, formats (the card's volume ID changes) and read errors. After an in-camera format, you confirm which card it is.
